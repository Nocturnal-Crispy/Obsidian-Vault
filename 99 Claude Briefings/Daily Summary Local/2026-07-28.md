#### Mercer County data-center zoning advances — countywide SALDO rules recommended
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Local`

- **What:** The Mercer County Regional Planning Commission has recommended a countywide SALDO amendment (Ordinance No. 2025-005) setting uniform data-center standards — dimensions, landscape buffers, noise/vibration, water/sewer, cooling, chemical handling, power, emergency management, and decommissioning. Commissioners can now accept, reject, or modify it, which would trigger a public hearing and a 45-day comment period. Affects 36 municipalities; Hermitage has a superseding ordinance.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Data-center siting shapes local land use, power demand, and economic-development dollars in the Shenango Valley. If you follow local grants/econ-dev, watch the commissioners' vote and the comment window.
