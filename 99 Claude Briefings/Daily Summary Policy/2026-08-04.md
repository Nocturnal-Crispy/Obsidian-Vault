#### 1099-NEC/MISC threshold rises to $2,000 for TY2026
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Policy`

- **What:** Beginning tax year 2026 the 1099-NEC/1099-MISC reporting threshold rises from $600 to $2,000 (indexed for inflation thereafter). Contractors paid $600–$1,999/yr will no longer trigger a 1099.
- **Why it matters (for you):** As a 1099 developer you'll issue and receive fewer forms; light-touch subcontractors under $2,000 drop off. Doesn't change income reporting obligations.
- **Source:** Littler — https://www.littler.com/news-analysis/asap/tax-bill-changes-1099-reporting-thresholds
