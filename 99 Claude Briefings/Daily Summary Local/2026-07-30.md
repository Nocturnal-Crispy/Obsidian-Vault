#### Data-center annexation fight escalates in Mercer County
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Local`

- **What:** A small Mercer County city council voted **3–2** to advance (first reading) an ordinance annexing farmland for a proposed hyperscale data center — enough land to roughly double the town.
