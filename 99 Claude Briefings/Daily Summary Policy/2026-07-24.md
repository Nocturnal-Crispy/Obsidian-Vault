#### OBBBA 1099 threshold: $600 → $2,000 for TY2026
`Heat: Low` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Policy`

- **What:** Both 1099-NEC and 1099-MISC reporting thresholds rise to **$2,000** for 2026 payments under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act. Income below the floor is still fully taxable and reportable — only the info-return trigger changed.
