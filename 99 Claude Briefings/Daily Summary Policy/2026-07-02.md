#### 1099 reporting overhaul takes effect for tax year 2026 (OBBBA)
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Policy/SmallBiz`

- **What:** Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, the 1099-NEC/1099-MISC reporting threshold rises from $600 to **$2,000** starting tax year 2026 (inflation-indexed from 2027). Form 1099-K reverts to the pre-2022 **$20,000 + 200 transactions** threshold.
- **Why it matters (for you):** As a 1099 consultant, you'll issue/receive fewer forms; don't assume "no 1099" means "no taxable income" — track all client payments regardless of threshold.
