#### 1099-NEC $2,000 threshold takes effect this tax year (TY2026)
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Policy/1099`

- **What:** For **tax year 2026**, payers only report non-employee payments once they total **$2,000+** in a year (up from $600), inflation-indexed starting 2027. IRS is also moving to **IRIS** as the primary information-return intake, retiring the legacy FIRE system for TY2026 filings.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Directly affects your 1099 consulting income and any 1099s you issue. The Battle Road Digital W9 → 1099 for TY2026 falls under these new rules. PA has its own direct filing requirements that don't follow federal extensions — verify state deadlines.
- **Source:** Tax1099 — https://www.tax1099.com/blog/new-1099-filing-requirements-every-business-should-know/ ; OnPay — https://onpay.com/insights/1099-reporting-threshold-updates/

#### Federal AI policy stays light-touch; state patchwork the real risk
`Heat: Low` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Policy/AI`

- **What:** Federal posture remains deregulatory (EO 14179 "Removing Barriers to American Leadership in AI" + OMB/Treasury guidance); no binding federal AI rules for small business. Compliance burden sits at the **state** level (CA rules 2026, CO effective 2027-01-01). EU AI Act Art. 50 transparency/labeling has been enforceable since Aug 2.
- **Why it matters (for you):** If you ship AI features to clients, the compliance surface is per-state (and EU if any customer is there) — build labeling/transparency in now rather than retrofit.
