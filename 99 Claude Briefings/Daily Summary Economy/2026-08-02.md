# Economy & Markets Intelligence — 2026-08-02

#### Fuel prices easing as crude falls — relief for input costs
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING (improving)` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Energy`

- **What:** EIA forecasts Q3 2026 gasoline averaging **~$3.80/gal**, down from >$4.20 in Q2; GasBuddy pegs 2026 diesel near **$3.55/gal**. Brent averaged ~$85/bbl in June (down ~$32 from the April peak) with further softening expected into 2027. Strait of Hormuz tanker traffic has resumed.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Lower diesel/gas eases farm input and equipment costs and any consulting travel. Falling energy also cools the inflation prints that were keeping the Fed cautious.
- **Source:** EIA STEO — https://www.eia.gov/outlooks/steo/

#### Fed on hold; inflation still the swing factor
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Macro`

- **What:** The Fed held rates (3.50–3.75%, 5th straight hold on 07-29) after May inflation spiked to ~4.2% on the earlier gasoline surge. It signaled additional hikes remain possible if inflation doesn't ease — but cheaper fuel now points the other way.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Borrowing costs stay elevated for equipment/expansion financing near-term; the energy pullback is the best case for rates drifting lower later in 2026.
- **Source:** NAHB / Financial World — https://www.nahb.org/blog/2026/07/fed-holds-rates-steady
