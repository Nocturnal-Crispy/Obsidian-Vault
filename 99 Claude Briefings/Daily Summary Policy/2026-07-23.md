#### AI rules harden from "ethics language" to enforceable duties — procurement is the near-term pressure point
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Policy`

- **What:** Mid-2026 coverage frames a shift from voluntary AI-ethics language to binding legal duties. No comprehensive federal AI statute; obligations sit in state law (CA, TX, IL, UT in force; CO's framework starts Jan 2027), with a federal-preemption push live. First real test for vendors is often procurement questionnaires — data sources, human review, logs, bias, liability — not regulators.
- **Why it matters (for you):** As a 1099 dev, expect client procurement teams (not agencies) to start asking how any AI you ship handles data, logging, and human review. Being able to answer cleanly is a differentiator.

#### 1099 reporting floor now $2,000 (was $600) for 2026 payments
`Heat: Low` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Small-biz/1099`

- **What:** Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, 1099-MISC/1099-NEC reporting minimums rose from $600 to $2,000 starting with 2026 payments. All income remains reportable on your return even below the floor.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Fewer 1099s issued to/by you for small jobs — but your income-reporting obligation is unchanged. Keep your own records; don't rely on receiving forms.
- **Source:** OnPay — https://onpay.com/insights/1099-reporting-threshold-updates/
