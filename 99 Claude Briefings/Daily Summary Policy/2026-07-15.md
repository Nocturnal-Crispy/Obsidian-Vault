#### 1099-NEC/MISC threshold rises to $2,000 for TY2026
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Policy`

- **What:** IRS proposed regs implement OBBBA: the $600 1099-NEC/MISC threshold rises to $2,000 per payee for payments on/after Jan 1, 2026, inflation-indexed thereafter. Contractors paid $600–$1,999 won't get a 1099 (income still taxable).
