# Policy Intelligence — 2026-07-22

#### OBBBA 1099 reporting threshold rises $600 → $2,000 (2026 payments)
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Policy/1099`

- **What:** Under the One Big Beautiful Bill Act, 1099-MISC/NEC reporting minimums rose from $600 to $2,000 starting with 2026 payments. All income is still taxable and reportable regardless of whether a 1099 is issued.
- **Why it matters (for you):** As both a payer (subcontractors) and payee, fewer 1099s will be issued — keep your own records; the reporting floor is not a tax-free floor.
- **Source:** OnPay — https://onpay.com/insights/1099-reporting-threshold-updates/

#### Utah sales tax now applies to digital products & prewritten software (eff. July 1, 2026)
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: NEW` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Policy/Tax`

- **What:** Utah expanded sales tax to digital products and prewritten (off-the-shelf) software effective July 1, 2026.
- **Why it matters (for you):** If you license software or SaaS to Utah customers, review nexus/collection obligations. A signal other states may follow.
- **Source:** Accounting Today — https://www.accountingtoday.com/news/states-introduce-ai-tax-changes

#### State AI laws in force; no federal statute; CO framework starts Jan 2027
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Policy/AI`
