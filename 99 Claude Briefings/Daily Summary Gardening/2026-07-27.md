# Gardening Intelligence — 2026-07-27 (zone 6a/6b)

#### Late blight risk rising with the wet week
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Gardening/Disease`

- **What:** Late blight (an oomycete hitting tomatoes/potatoes) thrives in humid, wet conditions — and the forecast is scattered storms today, 70% Tuesday. Penn State's late-blight alert system tracks county-level spore pressure.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Scout tomato and potato foliage for pale-green/brown lesions before and after the rain; improve airflow and avoid overhead watering. Catching it early is the whole game.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension — https://extension.psu.edu/2026-late-blight-updates

#### Tomato hornworm at peak
`Heat: Low` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Gardening/Pests`

- **What:** Hornworms are a July–early-September problem in PA and can strip sizeable portions of a tomato planting fast.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Hand-pick during your scouting pass; check undersides and stems.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension — https://extension.psu.edu/trees-lawns-and-landscaping/home-gardening/pests-and-diseases

#### Spotted lanternfly adults out
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Gardening/Pests`

- **What:** SLF adults (grey wings, black spots, red underwing) emerge July–October; PA quarantine covers most counties and pressure is pushing toward Mercer.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Kill adults on sight; scrape egg masses later in the season. Watch tree-of-heaven and grape.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension — https://extension.psu.edu/forage-and-food-crops/vegetables/pests-and-diseases
