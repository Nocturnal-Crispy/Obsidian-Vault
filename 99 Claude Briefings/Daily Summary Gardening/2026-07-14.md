#### Spotted lanternfly: adults emerging — July is peak control month in PA
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Pests`

- **What:** Nymphs are molting to adults through July; scout trees, vines (grape especially), patio furniture and structures. Report sightings: 1-888-4BADFLY. Mercer County is in the PA quarantine zone.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Early destruction now prevents heavy fall feeding on fruit/grape; adults are more mobile and harder to control later.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension / PA.gov (Tier 1) — https://extension.psu.edu/spotted-lanternfly-management-guide

#### Other July pests: Japanese beetles + tomato hornworm
`Heat: Low` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Pests`

- **What:** Japanese beetle adults active late June–July; tomato/tobacco hornworms a problem July–early September in PA.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Hand-pick beetles in early morning; check tomato foliage for hornworm frass/defoliation.
