# Gardening Intelligence — 2026-07-22 (Zone 6a/6b, Hermitage PA)

**Today's window:** Dry, high ~70°F, no frost — a good outdoor work day.

#### Top indeterminate tomatoes & strip lower leaves now
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: RISING (seasonal)` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Gardening`

- **What:** Late July–early August is the window to pinch the main leader above the top flower cluster (redirects energy to ripening) and strip leaves from the bottom 12–18" for airflow.
- **Why it matters (for you):** In zone 6 you want fruit ripening before first frost; airflow cuts early-blight pressure that peaks in August's humidity.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension — https://extension.psu.edu/forage-and-food-crops/vegetables/pests-and-diseases

#### Spotted lanternfly adults emerging — July is peak control month
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED (seasonal)` · `Tier 2 — Reputable` · `Category: Pests`

- **What:** SLF nymphs are completing their final molt into winged adults; July is the most critical control month in PA. Scout trees, vines, patio furniture, and structures.
- **Why it matters (for you):** PA remains under quarantine; knocking down adults now reduces egg-laying pressure this fall.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension / Positivebloom — https://positivebloom.com/why-july-is-the-most-critical-month-for-spotted-lanternfly-control-in-pennsylvania-gardens/
