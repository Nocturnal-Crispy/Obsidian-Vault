#### Langflow becomes the first AI-agent platform added to CISA's KEV catalog
`Heat: High` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Security`

- **What:** CISA added Langflow — an open-source visual builder for AI-agent / RAG pipelines — to the Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog.
