# Agriculture Intelligence — 2026-07-22

#### USDA July outlook: cattle/hog/poultry prices firm on tight supplies
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Livestock/Ag-Markets`

- **What:** USDA ERS July Livestock, Dairy & Poultry Outlook projects fed cattle, hogs, broilers, and turkey all stronger in 2026 than 2025 on strong demand and tight cattle supplies. Farm-level cattle prices rose 5.4% Apr→May and were 16.9% higher YoY in May; hogs forecast ~$69/cwt.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Firm output prices favor selling into strength; tight cattle supply keeps replacement/feeder costs elevated.
- **Source:** USDA ERS — https://www.ers.usda.gov/publications/115092

#### Feed costs keep falling — corn, soymeal, alfalfa lower
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: DECLINING (price)` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Crops/Ag-Markets`

- **What:** USDA expects continued declines in corn, soybean meal, and alfalfa hay prices; a record 2025/26 corn crop and higher soy crush are boosting feed availability.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Feed tailwind for any livestock — favorable window to book feed, but the rally that lifted prices is fading, so don't over-forward-buy.
- **Source:** USDA ERS / DTN — https://www.dtnpf.com/agriculture/web/ag/livestock/article/2026/02/19/livestock-poultry-prices-expected

#### HPAI pressure eased vs. 2025; eggs normalizing
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: DECLINING` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Livestock/Biosecurity`

- **What:** USDA reports fewer HPAI detections in Q1 2026 than Q1 2025; replacement pullet supply adequate. Egg production forecast +6% to 9.2B dozen; eggs projected ~$1.25/dozen assuming no major new outbreaks.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Biosecurity vigilance still warranted, but the acute enzootic pressure tracked earlier in July has softened per the latest outlook.
- **Source:** USDA APHIS / ERS — https://www.aphis.usda.gov/h5n1-hpai
