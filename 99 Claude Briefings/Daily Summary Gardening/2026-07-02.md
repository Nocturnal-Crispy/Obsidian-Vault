# Gardening Intelligence — Hermitage (Zone 6a/6b) — 2026-07-02

#### Extreme heat: prioritize deep, early watering
`Heat: High` · `Momentum: RISING` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Gardening`

- **What:** NWS Extreme Heat Warning through 10 PM July 3 (high ~96°F, heat index ~102). No frost risk.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Water deeply at dawn to reduce evaporation and heat stress; mulch to hold moisture; harvest ripe tomatoes/beans before midday to avoid heat splitting. Give transplants temporary afternoon shade.
- **Source:** NWS api.weather.gov (Tier 1)

#### Spotted lanternfly nymph season active in PA
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: SUSTAINED` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Pests`

- **What:** SLF nymphs are active mid-summer across PA's quarantine zone; feeding causes honeydew and sooty mold on vines, trees and garden plants.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Scout grape/tree-of-heaven and squish nymphs; check vehicles/trailers for egg masses before leaving the quarantine area. Report sightings: 1-888-4BADFLY.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension / PA.gov — https://www.pa.gov/agencies/pda/plants-land-water/spotted-lanternfly/spotted-lanternfly-alert

#### Tomato season pests: hornworm + blight in humid heat
`Heat: Medium` · `Momentum: NEW` · `Tier 1 — Confirmed` · `Category: Pests`

- **What:** July–September is prime tomato hornworm season in PA, and humid summers favor early/late blight.
- **Why it matters (for you):** Inspect tomato foliage every few days for hornworms and blight lesions; improve airflow, water at the base (not overhead), and remove affected lower leaves promptly.
- **Source:** Penn State Extension — https://extension.psu.edu/forage-and-food-crops/vegetables/pests-and-diseases
